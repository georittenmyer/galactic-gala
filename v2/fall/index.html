About Our Fundraiser

This fall fundraiser directly supports student programs, field trips, and enrichment activities at Salmon Bay K-8. Every contribution makes a meaningful difference in our students' educational experience.

How to Help

You can support our fundraiser by making a donation, participating in our events, or spreading the word to your community. All funds go directly to student programs.

Questions?

Contact the SBK8 PTA for more information about how your support helps our students. We're grateful for your partnership in education.

Getting Involved

We're creating multiple ways for community members to participate:

  • Direct donations to support student programs
  • Auction items and donor opportunities
  • Community events throughout the fall season
  • Volunteering opportunities
