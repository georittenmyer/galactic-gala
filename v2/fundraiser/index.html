Our Mission

SBK8 fundraising is dedicated to supporting the educational experience at Salmon Bay K–8. Through coordinated giving and community engagement, we bridge funding gaps and provide resources that enhance student learning and school community.

Our year-round approach includes seasonal campaigns and ongoing direct giving opportunities, ensuring sustainable support for our school's priorities and student programs.

Brand Guidelines

Color Palette

Black

#000000

White

#FFFFFF

Red (Accent)

#DC143C

Typography

Body font: Fredoka (friendly, rounded, approachable)

Heading font: Righteous (bold, playful, energetic)

Creates a young, fun, school-appropriate feel while staying professional.

Usage

  • Both logos represent the same mission and can be used interchangeably
  • Red accent can emphasize key messaging and calls-to-action
  • Maintain clear spacing around logos
  • Always pair with supporting text for context
