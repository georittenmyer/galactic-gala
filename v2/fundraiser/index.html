How Our Funding Works

Families and community members give twice per year. These donations fund FOSB's annual budget, which directly pays for Salmon Bay K–8. Our fundraising dollars bridge critical gaps left by district funding shortfalls—particularly in staff salaries and essential school programs.

Fall & Spring Giving

Direct donations from families and community members support FOSB's operating budget. These contributions are essential—they literally fund the school.

What Gets Funded

Staff support, student programs, field trips, enrichment activities, and school operations. Without community giving, these vital resources wouldn't exist.

About Enrichment Fees

Enrichment fees are separate from fundraising. These are required fees for specific programs (like electives or activities) and go directly to those accounts. Enrichment fees are mandatory, but scholarships are available for families that need support. Contact the school if cost is a barrier.
